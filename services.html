01 · Industrial

Industrial Water Treatment

Process and effluent water systems built for continuous plant operation — sized to your throughput, feed water quality, and discharge requirements.

Covers intake filtration, process water conditioning, and effluent/wastewater treatment before discharge or reuse. Systems are specified after a site water analysis so they match your actual load, not a generic package.

  • Site water testing and system sizing
  • Process water pre-treatment and conditioning
  • Effluent treatment for regulatory compliance
  • Installation, commissioning and operator handover
02 · Commercial

Commercial Water Treatment

Consistent water quality for offices, hospitality, healthcare and institutional buildings, where downtime isn't an option.

Sized for building-scale demand with an emphasis on reliability and low maintenance — filtration, softening and disinfection combined as the site requires.

  • Building-wide filtration and softening
  • Boiler and HVAC feed water treatment
  • Scheduled maintenance contracts available
  • Compact system options for space-limited plant rooms
03 · Residential

Residential (Domestic) Water Treatment

Whole-home and under-sink systems for households — clean drinking water and protected plumbing without an industrial footprint.

We recommend a system based on your source water (municipal, borewell, or tanker supply) rather than selling a one-size package.

  • Whole-house filtration
  • Under-sink drinking water (RO) systems
  • Water softeners for hard water areas
  • Installation and annual filter servicing
04 · Pools

Swimming Pool Systems

Filtration, circulation and sanitation systems for private and commercial pools, sized to pool volume and bather load.

Covers new pool system design as well as retrofits and upgrades to existing filtration and dosing setups.

  • Sand, cartridge and media filtration
  • Circulation pump sizing and installation
  • Chlorine, salt-chlorination or ozone sanitation
  • Automated dosing and water balance monitoring
05 · Ozone

Ozone Generators

Chemical-free disinfection using ozone (O₃) — strong oxidation power with no residual chemical taste or odour in treated water.

Suited to drinking water disinfection, pool sanitation, and process applications where chemical dosing needs to be reduced or eliminated.

  • Ozone generator sizing by flow and contact time
  • Integration with existing filtration systems
  • Replacement ozone cells and diffusers
  • Maintenance and output testing
06 · Dosing

Dosing Pumps & Chlorination Systems

Automated, accurately-metered chemical dosing for disinfection, pH correction and scale control.

Dosing pumps and chlorination skids sized to flow rate and required contact time, with manual or sensor-controlled operation.

  • Chlorination systems for disinfection
  • Metering/dosing pumps for pH and chemical control
  • Sensor-based automated dosing setups
  • Replacement pump heads, tubing and diaphragms
07 · Ultrafiltration

Ultrafiltration (UF)

Membrane filtration that physically removes suspended solids, bacteria and larger pathogens without chemical treatment.

Often used as a pre-treatment stage ahead of RO, or as a standalone system where full desalination isn't required.

  • UF membrane system design and sizing
  • Pre-treatment integration ahead of RO
  • Membrane cleaning and replacement
  • Standalone UF units for smaller sites
08 · Softening

Water Softeners

Ion-exchange softening that removes calcium and magnesium hardness, protecting pipework, boilers, appliances and fixtures from scale.

Sized to your water hardness level and daily consumption, with manual or automatic regeneration.

  • Hardness testing and system sizing
  • Automatic and manual regeneration softeners
  • Resin replacement and servicing
  • Industrial and residential capacities
09 · Reverse Osmosis

Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems

High-purity water production for drinking water, process water, and applications where dissolved solids need to be removed.

From single-outlet drinking water units to multi-stage industrial RO plants, sized to your feed water TDS and required output quality.

  • Drinking water RO systems (residential & commercial)
  • Industrial-scale RO plants
  • Membrane replacement and system servicing
  • TDS testing and performance checks
